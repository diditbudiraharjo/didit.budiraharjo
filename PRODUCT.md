# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective clients evaluating a creative/branding/development agency — founders, marketing leads, and product owners scouting a partner for a brand identity, website, or digital product. They arrive from a referral, portfolio search, or design-inspiration discovery (Awwwards-style browsing) and are implicitly judging the agency's own craft as a proof of capability: if the site itself isn't exceptional, the agency isn't trusted to deliver. Job to be done: quickly grasp what the agency does, feel its taste and technical caliber, see relevant proof (services, philosophy, work), and reach out.

## Product Purpose

"matte design" is a creative agency landing page covering three services: creative design, brand identities, and smart development. The site exists to convert visual/technical craft into trust — every scroll, transition, and 3D interaction is itself the pitch. Success = a visitor who scrolls the whole page, feels the premium/intelligent tone land, and starts a contact/quote conversation.

## Brand Personality

Tagline: "Design with Purpose. Build with Intelligence."
Three-word core: Minimal, Elegant, Intelligent.
Full essence set (from brand moodboard): Creative, Strategic, Intelligent, Reliable, Premium. Mood & tone: Minimal, Elegant, Professional, Innovative, Trustworthy.
Voice: confident, precise, unhurried — states capability without hype language or exclamation-heavy copy.
Reference sites (motion/3D/typographic caliber to match, not to copy): trionn.com, rolfjensen.studio, izanami-official.com, aboutluca.com — big confident typography, cinematic WebGL/3D hero, buttery scroll choreography, restrained monochrome palettes, magnetic/interactive micro-details.

## Anti-references

- Generic corporate/SaaS template scaffolding: identical icon+heading+text card grids, gradient-clip text, a tiny uppercase tracked eyebrow ("ABOUT" / "01 · PROCESS") repeated above every section, cheesy stock photography of people shaking hands in a boardroom.
- Busy, multi-color, gradient-heavy surfaces. The brand moodboard is a strict matte-black / warm-white monochrome system — color is not a design lever here, contrast/type/motion/space are.
- Static, non-interactive pages. Every section should carry deliberate scroll behavior, transition, or micro-interaction — this is an explicit brief requirement, not decoration.

## Design Principles

- Monochrome discipline: matte black (#0E0E0E) and warm white (#F7F6F3) carry the entire palette; hierarchy comes from type scale, spacing, opacity, and motion — never from added color.
- Motion is the pitch: 3D, scroll-linked, and pointer-linked interactions are core deliverables, not embellishment, but every one degrades gracefully to a calm reduced-motion equivalent.
- Confidence over decoration: large, restrained typography and generous negative space; no ornament that doesn't carry brand meaning (the signature "M" mark is the one recurring motif).
- Craft is the proof: the site's own build quality (smooth scroll, considered easing, real imagery, real content) is the strongest argument the agency can make for itself.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Every animation (3D logo, GSAP scroll sequences, Framer Motion reveals, cursor/marquee effects) must have a `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback (crossfade or instant state, never suppressed content). Keyboard-navigable nav/menu/forms with visible focus states, sufficient color contrast on the monochrome palette (body text ≥4.5:1), and semantic landmarks/headings throughout.
